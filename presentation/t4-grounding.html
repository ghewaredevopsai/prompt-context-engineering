The budget

The window is a budget, and you are already spending it

You will see this picture again in the Token Optimization module, annotated with what to evict first. It is the same budget seen from the other side: here it buys accuracy, there it buys money.

The red band is deliberately most of the bar. On the freight desk repository, "attach everything" is about forty-six thousand estimated tokens, and the two files that actually decide the answer are under three thousand. The lab measures exactly that, so the room does not have to take this on faith.

Three ways

Paste it, point at it, or let it go looking

Search is not a substitute for knowing. It is the right tool for an unfamiliar repository and the wrong one for the file you had open ten seconds ago. Most over-spending is somebody using column three for a column two problem.

The arrow runs the way it does on purpose: precision and cost fall together, which is not what people expect. They expect a trade-off, and reach for the expensive option believing they are buying accuracy with money. Lab 4 is fifteen minutes of evidence that they are not.
